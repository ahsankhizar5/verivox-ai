# Design System: VeriVox Acoustic Forensics
**Skills Applied:** `taste-skill` (`Leonxlnx/taste-skill`) + `hallmark` (`Nutlope/hallmark`)

## Configuration Dials
- `DESIGN_VARIANCE`: `7` (Asymmetric split hero, bento technical matrices, structured editorial grids)
- `MOTION_INTENSITY`: `5` (Restrained, purposeful transitions, tactile button states, zero pulsing blur orbs)
- `VISUAL_DENSITY`: `6` (Balanced gallery breathing room on marketing pages; high-clarity forensic cockpit density in the dashboard)

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
VeriVox is a bootstrapped AI startup and acoustic forensics platform built for Expo demonstration and real-world audio verification (`verivox.tech`). The interface rejects generic SaaS AI clichés in favor of a **Precision Forensic Instrument** aesthetic: warm alabaster and crisp white surfaces paired with deep carbon-zinc panels, `1px` structural hairlines, and monospace acoustic telemetry.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Alabaster Canvas** (`#FAFAFA`) — Primary page background
- **Pure Surface** (`#FFFFFF`) — Elevated inspection cards and interactive modules
- **Carbon Obsidian** (`#09090B`) — High-contrast dark panels, hero console, auth showcase, and primary ink
- **Zinc Slate** (`#52525B`) — Body copy and technical descriptions
- **Muted Telemetry** (`#71717A`) — Captions, coordinates, and secondary labels
- **Hairline Border** (`#E4E4E7` / `rgba(24,24,27,0.08)`) — Crisp `1px` structural dividers
- **Signal Cobalt** (`#0284C7`) — Primary interactive accent and focus rings
- **Bona Fide Emerald** (`#10B981`) — Authentic speech verification indicator
- **Spoof Crimson** (`#EF4444`) — Synthetic / cloned audio alert indicator
- **Inconclusive Amber** (`#F59E0B`) — Out-of-distribution / low-confidence boundary warning

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Display & UI Sans:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`--font-sans`) — Tight tracking (`-0.03em`) on headings, clean geometric legibility for UI controls.
- **Technical Monospace:** `JetBrains Mono` (`--font-mono`) — Mandatory for all acoustic metrics (`16 kHz`, `64,600 samples`, `EER 2.239`), confidence scores, timestamps, file sizes, and section indices (`01 // ARCHITECTURE`).

## 4. Hallmark Anti-Slop Floor (Strict Bans)
- **Zero Unverified Claims:** No fake user counts (`10K+ users`), fake file counts (`2M+ files`), fake country counts (`120+ countries`), unverified accuracy numbers (`99.2%`), fake compliance certifications (`SOC 2`, `ISO 27001`), or fake physical addresses (`123 Tech Street, San Francisco`).
- **Zero Emojis:** No emojis in headings, badges, or cards.
- **Zero Floating Sparkle Pills:** Use monospace section indices instead of rounded pill badges with sparkle icons.
- **Zero Blurred Pulsing Blobs:** No `blur-3xl animate-pulse` decorative background circles.
